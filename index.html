How to choose

Pick by how you actually work

Token plans are not the same product. Some sell monthly credits, some sell rolling 5-hour windows, some sell an IDE. Match the meter to your rhythm.

01

Burst vs steady

Night owls and weekend warriors often prefer monthly credit pools (MiMo, StepFun). Daily grinders may like 5-hour rolling windows (MiniMax, GLM).

02

One model vs many

Vendor packs lock you into a family. Aggregators like OpenCode Go and Qwen Coding Plan let you hop models without juggling five keys.

03

Agent quality bar

Claude remains the quality ceiling for many workflows. Open and China-native plans chase that bar at lower cost — great for volume, drafts, and side projects.

FAQ

Straight answers

What is a “token plan” or coding plan?

A fixed monthly (or annual) subscription that gives you included usage for AI coding models inside tools like Claude Code, OpenCode, Cline, or Cursor — instead of pure pay-per-token API billing.

Does Scouty sell these plans?

No. This is an independent informational guide. We don’t process payments or sell subscriptions. When you’re ready, use the official provider links to subscribe on their site.

Are prices guaranteed?

No. Vendors change tiers, promos, and regional pricing often. This guide is a curated snapshot as of July 2026. Always confirm current pricing on the provider’s official page.

Can I use these inside Cursor?

Many plans expose OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible endpoints you can point Cursor at. Cursor’s own Pro/Ultra plans are a separate product: an IDE with bundled usage.

Which plan is cheapest to start?

OpenCode Go ($5 first month), Xiaomi MiMo Lite ($6), and StepFun Flash Mini ($6.99) are common entry points. Match the model quality you need, not just the sticker price.
